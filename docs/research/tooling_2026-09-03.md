# Research digest — tooling — 2026-09-03 (pass 4)

## 1. EVALUATE (narrow scope only) — `RhinoSecurityLabs/GCPBucketBrute`
- **What it is:** Established pentest firm (Rhino Security Labs) tool, 573★/91 forks. Given a bucket name, checks GCS bucket permissions **unauthenticated** (or optionally with creds) and flags privilege-escalation-capable ACLs. Its headline mode is keyword→**permutation-generation**→blind scan.
- **The gap it could fill:** our bucket lane (`recon_bucket_scanner.sh`) is S3Scanner-only — **no GCS reader at all**. When JS-intel/params mining turns up a provenance-confirmed `storage.googleapis.com/<bucket>` reference (not uncommon on GCP-hosted targets — GCP is already in our top-tech list), we currently have nothing to grade its ACL with.
- **Doctrine conflict — the part to REJECT:** its core permutation-brute mode is exactly the anti-pattern our own KB (`class-bucket-exposure.md`) calls out — "we DON'T blind-permute names (saturated dup-magnet + third-party-data risk)." Do not run its keyword-generation feature.
- **Verdict: EVALUATE, single-bucket-check mode only** — worth wiring as a GCS-ACL reader invoked *only* on a provenance-confirmed bucket name already surfaced from a target's own JS/params (same gate as our S3 pipeline: `--provenance` required to mint). Never invoke its name-generation permutation feature — that would violate the lane's core safety design.
