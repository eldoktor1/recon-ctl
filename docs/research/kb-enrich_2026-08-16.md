## 1. Race conditions — first-sequence-sync breaks the ~30-request single-packet ceiling (class-race-conditions)
Our doc only has the basic HTTP/2 single-packet Turbo Intruder script. New research (GMO Flatt Security, Aug 2024) extends it well past the practical limits that cap most bug-bounty race testing — directly relevant to `/verify`/`/limit` brute-force-style races where 20-30 synced requests aren't enough attempts to land a statistically reliable hit (e.g. bypassing a 5-attempt OTP lockout needs hundreds of tries, not 20).

## 2. SSRF — concrete encoding payloads + missing cloud providers (class-ssrf)
Our doc reasons about bypass *classes* (userinfo/fragment/encoding) without example payloads, and its cloud-metadata table only lists AWS/GCP/Azure. Added concrete payloads + Alibaba/Oracle/DigitalOcean endpoints, an open-redirect→metadata chaining pattern, and a distinct "forged Host header trusted for server-side proxying" SSRF-adjacent gadget class seen in recent Next.js CVEs.
