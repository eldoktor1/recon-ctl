## Tooling Watch — 2026-07-28 (supplemental #3)

### ADOPT — Smugglex (hahwul/smugglex) — request-smuggling detection
Rust-powered scanner covering CL.TE / TE.CL / TE.TE **and** HTTP/2 vectors we have no current tool for: H2C smuggling and H2→H1 downgrade desync (`h2-downgrade` check). Actively maintained (321 commits, ongoing PRs). CLI: `smugglex https://target.com`, structured output for pipeline integration.
- **Why it fills a real gap:** we have `docs/knowledge/class-request-smuggling.md` but no dedicated detection primitive in any lane — smuggling checks are currently ad-hoc/manual. Differential/timing-based smuggling probes are inherently unauth-safe and non-destructive (desync detection, not exploitation) — fits doctrine directly.
- **Fit:** unauth-safe ✅, dup-resistant ✅ (few crowd hunters run dedicated smuggling scanners at scale), low burn risk if rate-limited like our other active-probe lanes.
- **Verdict: ADOPT** — worth wiring into a `recon_smuggling.sh`-style lane (same shape as `recon_wcd.sh`: unauth-safe LEAD surfacer, real desync confirmation stays a careful/rate-limited step, never destructive replay against production).
