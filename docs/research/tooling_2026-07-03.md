# Research digest — tooling — 2026-07-03

## Tooling Watch — 2026-07-03

Surveyed 8 lanes, ~15 searches. Three genuine ADOPTs/EVALUATEs; four SKIPs with reasons. Highest-value first.

---

### ADOPT — Subdominator (takeover lane upgrade)
**https://github.com/Stratus-Security/Subdominator** · 310★ · last release v1.72 Sept 2024

97 service fingerprints vs nuclei's ~72 takeover templates. Key edge: **traces the full CNAME chain to the terminal A record** — other tools (including BadDNS) stop at first hop. Also matches A/AAAA records directly (catches IP-only takeovers). Dynamic Azure validators cut FPs. ~8x faster than Subjack/Subdover at scale.

**Fits doctrine:** unauth, read-only DNS only. Complements BadDNS (second-order NS/MX delegation lane stays). Wire into the takeover pass after subfinder resolves, alongside BadDNS.

---

### EVALUATE — Vespasian / Praetorian (API surface discovery)
**https://github.com/praetorian-inc/vespasian** · 106★ · v1.0.0 Apr 2026 (brand new)

Wire-level SPA traffic capture + static JS bundle re-analysis in one tool. Watches what the browser *actually fires* (not guesses from wordlists like kiterunner) then cross-references with static AST scan for routes that never get exercised in a default crawl. Outputs OpenAPI/GraphQL SDL spec → feeds directly into our IDOR ranker and GraphQL worklist.

**Gap vs existing:** kiterunner brute-forces paths; jsluice mines JS text; Vespasian observes live requests *and* re-scans captured bundles. Complements both without replacing either. Unauthed crawl mode supported; read-only.

**Caveat:** 106 stars, no track record. SPA-heavy apps will show thin unauthed surface. Trial on 3-5 API-heavy targets before wiring to daemon.

Blog: https://www.praetorian.com/blog/vespasian-api-endpoint-discovery-tool/

---

### EVALUATE — pphack (prototype pollution → DOM-XSS pipeline)
**https://github.com/edoardottt/pphack** · 250★ · v0.1.4 May 2026 (572 commits, actively maintained)

Headless-Chromium prototype pollution scanner. Injects pollution payloads and detects DOM-level impact dynamically — dalfox, kxss, and gf-patterns are all blind to this class. A confirmed PP + gadget = XSS PoC without direct injection = low dup-risk.

**Fits doctrine:** GET-only headless, unauthed, non-destructive. Detection mode only (no `-e` flag in autonomous use). Feed hits to `dalfox --deep-domxss --force-headless-verification` for EXECUTION confirmation — PP hit alone is LEAD, dalfox execution is CONFIRMED.

**Extension point:** bolt onto `recon-domxss` as a pre-pass before the source→sink miner.

---

### EVALUATE — x8 (hidden param, value-bearing)
**https://github.com/Sh1Yo/x8** · 2.1k★ · last release May 2023 (maintenance unclear)

Rust param brute-forcer with a technique arjun lacks: **detects params that require a specific value** (e.g. `admin=true`, `debug=1`) by doing line-by-line page comparison rather than structural diff only. Faster than arjun at scale (Rust vs Python). GET/POST/JSON/XML/header modes.

**Fits doctrine:** unauth GET/POST, rate-limited. No auth.

**Caveat:** No release since May 2023. May be feature-complete rather than dead. Test alongside arjun as optional second pass for `recon-params arjun`; don't wire autonomously until maintenance confirmed.

---

### SKIP — Cariddi
3.4k★, active, but regex-only JS scanning (not AST). Our katana + jsluice + trufflehog pipeline is deeper. No genuine gap filled; well-known = not dup-resistant.

### SKIP — Hadrian (Praetorian BOLA tester)
Requires pre-configured auth creds for both roles and creates/deletes resources during testing. Doctrine-incompatible for autonomous use. Our IDOR lane is correctly human-in-the-loop.

### SKIP — WSHawk (WebSocket injection)
9 stars, no community validation, active injection payloads = not autonomous-safe. The WebSocket lane gap is real but this tool isn't the answer.

---

### Lanes Still Genuinely Absent

- **WebSocket recon** — no passive-detect-only tool with real community validation exists yet. Gap confirmed, watch for something at 200+ stars.
- **OAuth/OIDC flow recon** — no autonomous-safe tool for PKCE-bypass, token-via-referer, or open-redirect in OAuth flows. High-payout class, research-only for now.
